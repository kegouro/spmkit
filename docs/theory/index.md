# Theory guide

SPM-Kit includes a study guide on the physical foundations of scanning probe
microscopy (AFM / KPFM), covering the theoretical concepts that underpin the
analyses implemented in the software.

## Chapters

1. [AFM principles](afm-principles.md) — what the AFM is, the optical lever, feedback
2. [Operating modes](operating-modes.md) — contact, tapping, non-contact
3. [Force-distance curves](force-distance.md) — approach, retract, snap-in, pull-off
4. [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md) — Hertz, Sneddon, reduced modulus
5. [KPFM](kpfm.md) — contact potential difference, work function
6. [Roughness & spectral analysis](roughness.md) — ISO 25178, PSD, Hurst, fractal dimension
7. [Resonance & mass sensing](resonance.md) — thermal tuning, Q factor, mass sensing
8. [SPM-Kit workflows](spmkit-workflows.md) — from concept to software
9. [Glossary](glossary.md)
10. [References](references.md)

Each chapter connects the physics to the corresponding SPM-Kit implementation,
including the exact module path and evidence status.
