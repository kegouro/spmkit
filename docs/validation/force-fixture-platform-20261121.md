### Contact mechanics

M01 differed at zero-based samples 140, 151 and 162. The separation
and force coefficient agree. At index 162, the waveforms give
`0x1.2816a9cfa91ccp-29` N frozen and `0x1.2816a9cfa91cdp-29` N
regenerated. Evaluating the power of the exact binary64 indentation in
70-digit decimal precision, rounding to binary64, and applying the original
force coefficient gives the regenerated value at all three indices.
One downward ULP at the power reproduces the frozen values with the same
multiplication. The same Hertz term propagates into M06, M07, M09–M13 and
M17. M04 subtracts adhesion from the Hertz term. Its physical force error
is the same size as M01's, while cancellation increases the ULP distance.
Subtracting the recorded 2 nN adhesion from the corresponding M01 array
reproduces M04 exactly in both the frozen and regenerated versions.

M05 uses a grid of JKR contact radii. Its indentation subtracts terms in
`a²/R - c√a` and the force subtracts terms containing `a³`. Replaying
the interpolations with adjacent binary64 values at the squared or cubed
grid endpoints reproduces the six frozen ordinates. For example, decreasing
the upper endpoint's cube by one ULP reproduces sample 183 at the same
interpolation bracket. These tests locate the sensitivity to rounding in the
JKR tabulation. The frozen archive records the resulting force values, so
the original native kernel and its intermediate grid were not identified.

### Viscoelasticity

V01 has five changed ramp samples and eight changed hold samples. Its time
axis and geometry are exact. Computing the ramp power and relaxation
exponential in 70-digit decimal precision and rounding each to binary64
reproduces the macOS and Ubuntu 3.12 force. Replaying a one-ULP downward
change to the ramp power or exponential reproduces the five frozen ramp
values. In the hold the power is absent and decreasing the exponential by
one ULP reproduces all eight frozen values with the original multiply and
divide. The other lumped and power-law cases V02–V07, V10 and V12–V23 also
show at most two ULP of force rounding. The noisy V11 case has a distinct
convolution component described below.

For Lee–Radok (V08) and Ting (V09), the indentation histories use powers
0.7 and 0.8. Decreasing the power by one ULP reproduces every changed
separation sample (V08 index 175 and V09 indices 65, 151, 162 and 220).
The hereditary integral forms differences of indentation powers and sums
substeps weighted by an exponential modulus. Ting also interpolates the
loading history. These operations propagate roundoff into the force and
from there into the height. The recovered logs identify force deviations
through the integration path and the affected geometry values. They give
no native-kernel trace for each quadrature substep.

### Correlated noise

M07 and V11 originally used `np.convolve` to apply five identical weights
of 0.2. With the same seeded samples and same window order, explicit
left-to-right fused multiply-add reproduces the extra macOS interior
differences. Rounding each product and addition separately reproduces the
Ubuntu 3.12 archives byte for byte when applied on macOS. Relative and ULP
differences grow near cancellation in the small noise samples. For
example, before the correction the extra absolute difference introduced
by this kernel was at most 4.039e-28 N for M07 and 2.019e-28 N for V11.

Both generators now specify the separate multiplication and sequential
sum with zero padding. The frozen V11 archive has two edge values that
agree with fused evaluation of the truncated windows. They are one ULP
from the chosen consistent split evaluation. After the change, M07 has
three remaining Hertz differences (1 ULP maximum) and V11 has seven
remaining differences (2 ULP maximum, including its relaxation term).
Regression tests cover the pure noise before contact, including the
truncated windows.

### Single-molecule force spectroscopy

For S19, only rupture-force index 42 changed. The relevant CDF bracket in
the second loading-rate series is grid indices 3083–3084. The left CDF
value is `0x1.ff3a11e3c9d02p-5` with Apple libSystem and
`0x1.ff3a11e3c9d01p-5` when only the kernel for the powers of `z` in
the DHS rate is replaced with Arm optimized-routines `pow`. This
substitution reproduces the frozen rupture-force series, including
`0x1.f79105383119ep-34` N at index 42. Swapping the rate or survival
`exp` alone leaves the macOS result `0x1.f79105383119dp-34` N. Both
fused and split final interpolation yield the same result for each
bracket. The Arm probe was built with implicit contraction disabled
and its explicit-FMA option tested both ways.
[Arm scalar pow implementation](https://github.com/ARM-software/optimized-routines/blob/20859d3fd9e797dde592c489c8ef7d383f778620/math/pow.c)

S21 differs in indices 43 and 46 of `-log(1-u)/2`. The seeded uniforms,
censoring flags and force levels agree. At index 43 the exact input to
the logarithm is `0x1.ed9d880614916p-1`. The frozen lifetime is
`0x1.2b90bd9145168p-6` s and the regenerated lifetime is
`0x1.2b90bd9145169p-6` s. Evaluating the logarithm at 70-digit precision
and converting to binary64 yields the regenerated result for both S21
indices. S22 index 38 shows the opposite behavior. The frozen and Apple
values match rounded decimal, and the Arm scalar log probe reproduces
the Ubuntu 3.12 result one ULP below them. The division by two is exact
for these normal samples.
[Arm scalar log implementation](https://github.com/ARM-software/optimized-routines/blob/20859d3fd9e797dde592c489c8ef7d383f778620/math/log.c)

Ubuntu 3.11 and 3.12 had the same recorded `hypot` libm identity and
dispatch offset. They used different NumPy builds. NumPy's double
`power`, `exp` and `log` have native scalar and SIMD routes, so pinning
the wheel version cannot guarantee correctly rounded transcendentals on
every backend. The historical CI logs do not identify which loop each
of these ufuncs took. The operation-level probes distinguish their
rounding effects from convolution FMA. The fixture generators do not
call SciPy or BLAS directly for the affected model equations. The
existing model recovery, geometry, censoring and external-oracle
checks provide separate scientific constraints.
[NumPy power dispatch](https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.5.1/numpy/_core/src/umath/loops_umath_fp.dispatch.c.src),
[NumPy exponential and logarithm dispatch](https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/v2.5.1/numpy/_core/src/umath/loops_exponent_log.dispatch.c.src)

## Validation policy

The original JSON and NPZ files remain the reference. Two fresh
regenerations must still match each other byte for byte. Comparisons with
the frozen NPZ now check the array inventory, dtype, shape and values.
Signed zeros, NaN and infinity encodings and all undeclared channels are
exact. Mechanics and viscoelasticity JSON remains byte-exact. SMFS JSON
allows one ULP only for the three affected numeric lists. All other
manifest content is exact. The mirrored NPZ channels have the same limits.

The nonzero per-channel budgets are 2 ULP for Hertz-derived force, 16 ULP
for the DMT adhesion subtraction and 32 ULP for the JKR tabulation. The
last two bounds cover the measured cancellation amplification (16 and
29 ULP) without adding an absolute floor. The viscoelasticity limits are
4 ULP for lumped and power-law force (maximum 3) and 16 ULP for
Lee–Radok and Ting force (maxima 12 and 9). Only their derived height
and separation receive 1 ULP. SMFS has an adjacent-value limit of 1 ULP.
These are empirical binary64 limits for these particular fixtures and
operation sequences. They are neither global analysis tolerances nor
bounds for other inputs or arbitrary libm implementations. The scientific
fit, oracle and recovery criteria retain their existing limits.

Regression tests exercise the exact noise calculation, the one-ULP
boundary and rejection of changed type, shape, zero, sign or nonfinite
values. They also retain the three geometry and inventory checks against
the live generators.
